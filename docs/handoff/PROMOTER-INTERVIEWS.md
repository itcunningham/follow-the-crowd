## Respondent A — Sheets / club-night runner

1. **Ops:** Google Sheets — share with partners, organised format, set times, DJs, confirmed or not; bigger nights have a set-fee column.  
2. **Booking outreach:** Instagram easiest; bigger names → email agents (more complicated).  
3. **Tools:** Google Sheets; basic accounting on another sheet; later “proper” accounting software.  
4. **Hard part:** Not one thing — many small things; relies on DJs, venue, door staff; one fuck-up makes it harder.  
5. **Updates:** Always a **group chat for every club night/event**; info goes there.  
6. **Least fun:** Accounting — behind-the-scenes money boring but required. Also listed: contact venues, DJs, Instagram ads, check venue, set up, sound check.

---

## Respondent B — Google Docs + Drive crew

2. **Book / track / pay:** Google Doc with list of DJs and confirmations. Google Drive for DJ press kits.  
3. **Apps/tools:** Instagram + ads. Turn off AI settings on ads (they turn on and mess campaigns). **No other organising tools.**  
4. **Biggest headache:** Contacting venues / availability — venues slow; lock venue first.  
5. **When details change:** Contact every person, check availabilities, be apologetic, communicate fast.  
6. **Make easier:** Social media work; less stress about same-day ticket buys (people don’t buy early).

## Respondent C — Jaydn McRae (`jaydosss__`)

- Running events is hard; lots of competition.  
- **Hardest:** getting people to buy tickets — you’re selling a product; research; DJs/staff who can read a crowd.  
- Good brands take time; be decent; give back while filling your own pocket.  
- Not quick cash; years before real money; don’t piss off other promoters/clubs; stay in your lane.  
- Open to sit down / partner later.  
- Don’t overspend on big-name DJs without a crowd; build reliable **local resident** team; pay locals fairly ($50 is bullshit — they bring the crowd).  
- Marketing: Instagram, Facebook, targeted ads.  
- Need a **strong reliable promoter team** (not one post then expect pay).  
- Everything falls into place with a good team; hardest manual work is reaching people and getting them to show up.  
- Comms structure: **separate group chats** — one for promoters, one for owners/managers/door.

## Respondent D — Matthew Yetton (`matthew.yetton`)

- Some experience, not claiming pro.  
- Bookings often via **agents** who manage payments.  
- Register **ABN / business** for legal/tax.  
- Build a **brand** (Instagram etc.).  
- Reach venues; liaise agents/managers; AI useful for drafting contracts / pay timing.  
- Sub-promoters: **$ per ticket** or free tickets by volume — $ per ticket usually better incentive.  
- Offered further questions.

## Respondent E — brand / booking approach (earlier chat)

- Got in by helping promote nights, going to nights, talking to promoters.  
- Books people who **support the brand** and want growth.  
- Hard talking to artists; stays clear on why chosen / when / genre.  
- Payments via partners; pay other fees before yourself.  
- Promoting must be planned; **make sure you have promoters**.
