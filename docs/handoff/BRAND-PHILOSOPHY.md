# FTC Brand & Product Philosophy

**Status:** source of truth for product, UX, branding, marketing, and feature decisions.  
**Audience:** every Builder, Reviewer, QA agent, and ChatGPT planning session.  
**Related:** roadmap / GTM / revenue → `PRODUCT-VISION.md` · visual tokens → `docs/design/FTC_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` · what shipped → `CURRENT-STATE.md`

When making product, UX, branding, marketing, or feature decisions, always use this philosophy as the source of truth.

---

## Mission

FTC exists to remove everything that prevents culture from thriving.

We believe every unforgettable event starts with people working behind the scenes — promoters, event planners, DJs, venues, photographers, videographers, lighting crews, security, artists, and everyone else who contributes to live events.

Too much of their time is wasted by fragmented communication, unnecessary admin, and inefficient workflows. Every minute spent fighting software is a minute not spent creating culture.

FTC exists to remove that friction.

**For the Culture.**

---

## Who we start with

We start with **independent promoters and their crews** — then widen.

The long-term crowd includes venues, agencies, production, and the wider live-events industry. Day one is Melbourne promoters running real nights with less chaos.

---

## The Common Enemy

The enemy is **friction**.

Not competitors.

Not other apps.

The enemy is everything that gets in the way of creating great events:

- Scattered communication
- Endless chats across Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger, and email (not messaging itself — FTC DMs and Crew Chat fight this)
- Last-minute chaos
- Missed opportunities
- Administrative overload
- Broken workflows
- Tools that don’t work together

Every feature should fight this enemy.

---

## What “Follow the Crowd” Means

“Follow the Crowd” has two meanings:

1. Follow the people creating culture.
2. Join the community that creates unforgettable experiences.

The crowd is not just the audience.

The crowd is everyone who makes culture happen.

FTC helps turn ideas into crowds.

---

## Product Principles

Before proposing any feature, ask:

- Does this reduce friction?
- Does this help culture happen?
- Does this save meaningful time?
- Does this improve collaboration?
- Does this make creating events easier?

Then the proof question:

- Would a Melbourne promoter use this on a real Saturday night?

If the answer is no, don’t build it.

FTC should remain intentionally focused. Avoid feature bloat.

---

## Design Philosophy

Every screen should feel purposeful.

Every interaction should remove effort.

Every feature should have a clear reason to exist.

Complexity is the enemy.

Simplicity creates better experiences.

---

## Brand Voice

FTC is not just software.

It is the infrastructure behind culture.

We are building the operating system for live events.

Our purpose is not to manage events.

Our purpose is to help more great events exist.

Everything we design, write, or build should reinforce this mission.

**For the Culture.**
